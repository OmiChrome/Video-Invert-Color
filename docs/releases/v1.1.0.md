**Midnight Owl 1.1.0: a new engine.**

- **Smooth at any speed.** Every frame is processed on the GPU (WebGL2). Fast cuts never flash, even at 2.5×.
- **Smarter dark mode.** Only the paper and the lines on it flip. Boxes, icons, colour fills, hair, faces, photos, maps and illustrated scenes keep their look. No glow around boxes, white glyphs inside badges stay white, thick lines stay solid.
- **Grey and unevenly lit boards** go fully dark too.
- **Toolbar owl** now reliably lights up while it is darkening a video (it used to stay grey on YouTube).
- **Simpler popup**, plus **Diagnostics**: on-video stats (fps, render time, dropped frames) and a log you can save and share.
- Hides YouTube's ambient glow while on. Works on Firefox for Android.
