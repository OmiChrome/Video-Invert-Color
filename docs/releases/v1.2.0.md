**Midnight Owl 1.2.0: text comes first.**

- **Every letter is readable.** Text and drawings on a whiteboard or slide flip as a whole, black or coloured, bold or thin. No more half-flipped, hatched letters. Light text on near-black reaches about 12:1 contrast.
- **Clean edges.** Boxes, charts, icons and pictures that stay as they are now sit on the dark paper with no light outline and no grey halo. White symbols inside icons stay white.
- **Cartoons and drawings** go fully dark around big figures; outlines, eyes and mouths turn light together.
- **Illustrated scenes** stay natural. Only their white caption bars and speech bubbles turn dark, right up to the edge. The smudge that could appear under a caption bar is gone.
- Still light on old integrated GPUs: all analysis runs on a small 240×135 grid, and frames are processed at most 30 times a second by default.
