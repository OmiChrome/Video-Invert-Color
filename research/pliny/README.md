# Research frames: Sam O'Nella Academy, "Pliny Explains it All 2: MAN"

13 frames (1920×1080) from a real lecture-style video, used to tune the engine: hand-drawn figures on white paper,
grey backgrounds, coloured illustrated scenes, speech bubbles, caption bars, icons and small serif text.

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `frames/` | Original frames, named by their time in the video (`p_93.png` = 93 s) |
| `v1.2/` | The same frames through the v1.2 engine (Smart mode, brightness 0.8), at full size |
| `comparison-v1.2.jpg` | Every frame, original and v1.2 side by side |

To look at them through the current engine, serve the folder that holds both `extension/` and these frames and
render them with `OwlGL` (see the visual tuning loop in `CLAUDE.md`), or open them in the lab.
