OriginalMidnight Owl

What it does

Boards and slides go dark

Ink turns light and keeps its colour.

Faces stay human

People, photos and drawings are never inverted.

No flash at 2.5×

Every frame is decided on its own, so cuts stay dark.

Grey and badly lit boards too

Uneven lighting is evened out before the flip.

How it works

Only the paper flips

Bright, colourless backgrounds go dark. The lines drawn on them go light.

Everything else stays

Boxes, icons, colour fills, faces and photos keep their look, with highlights softened.

On the GPU, every frame

A tiny analysis grid plus one pass at screen size. No lag, even at 2.5×.

Nothing leaves your browser

No account, no tracking, no network requests. Settings stay on your device.

Controls

  • OOn or off, on any page with a video
  • AltOSame, from anywhere in the browser
  • Bright when it's working, grey when it isn't
  • PopupSmart · Invert · Dim, brightness, warm tint
